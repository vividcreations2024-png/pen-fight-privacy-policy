# Privacy Policy for Pen Fight

**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

**Developer / Publisher:** Vivid Creations
**Application Name:** Pen Fight
**Package Name:** `com.vividcreations.penfight`
**Contact Email:** vividcreations2024\@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Pen Fight** ("we", "our", or "us"). Vivid Creations respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information associated with your use of Pen Fight.

Pen Fight is an offline-first physics game. The game does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to play.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored locally by the game and what information may be processed by third-party services used to provide advertising and related functionality.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

Pen Fight does not operate its own user-account or backend profile system.

We do not directly collect or require:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- Your physical address
- Your contacts
- Your camera
- Your microphone
- Your photos or personal files
- Your precise GPS location
- A username or password

We do not sell personal information.

## 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Pen Fight stores game and preference information locally on your Android device using Android Jetpack DataStore.

This may include:

- Campaign level completion and star records
- Unlocked pens and cosmetic colorways
- Ink Drops balance
- Equipped pen and related game preferences
- Sound effects volume
- Classroom ambience volume
- Haptic feedback preference
- Game difficulty
- Other game-progress and configuration settings

This information is used to provide the game's functionality and progression.

Vivid Creations does not operate a server that receives this locally stored game-progress information.

If you clear the application's storage or uninstall Pen Fight, locally stored game data may be deleted or reset.

## 4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Pen Fight uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements.

Because advertising is provided by Google and its advertising partners, those services may process information associated with your device, app usage, and advertising interactions. The information processed may vary depending on factors such as your device, region, consent choices, and advertising configuration.

Examples may include:

- Advertising identifiers
- IP address
- Device and operating-system information
- App and advertising interaction information
- Advertisement impressions and interactions
- General network and diagnostic information

Vivid Creations does not control the information independently collected and processed by Google for advertising purposes.

For more information, please review Google's policies:

- [Google Advertising Technologies Privacy Information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Pen Fight uses Google's **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** where required to provide applicable privacy and consent choices.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be presented with a consent or privacy-choice dialog relating to advertising.

Where available, you can review or change your privacy choices from within Pen Fight:

1. Open **Settings** from the main menu.
2. Open **Game Info & Privacy**.
3. Select **Privacy Preferences**.

Your available choices may depend on your location and applicable privacy requirements.

You can also manage advertising-related settings through your Android device settings.

## 6. Android Permissions

Pen Fight uses the following Android permissions:

### `android.permission.INTERNET`

Used to allow third-party services such as Google AdMob and the consent framework to communicate over the internet.

### `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`

Used to determine network connectivity for functionality that requires an internet connection.

### `android.permission.VIBRATE`

Used to provide optional tactile feedback during gameplay and other game interactions. Haptic feedback can be controlled through the game's Settings where applicable.

Pen Fight does not require permissions for contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or personal files.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Pen Fight is intended for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Because Pen Fight uses third-party advertising services, advertising and data-processing practices may vary according to the user's location, age-related settings, consent status, and applicable policies.

Parents or guardians who have questions or concerns about privacy or information processing may contact us using the contact information below.

## 8. Data Retention and Security

Game-progress and preference information stored locally by Pen Fight remains on the user's device unless the application data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Vivid Creations does not maintain a backend user-profile database for Pen Fight.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is subject to those providers' own privacy policies, security practices, and retention policies.

We recommend reviewing Google's privacy documentation for information about Google's handling of advertising-related data.
